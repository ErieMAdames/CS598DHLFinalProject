Hey everyone! For the final project, with the help of an AI assistant, your team will need to reproduce the results for a healthcare research paper from a predetermined list below. Since we recognize that generative models (i.e ChatGPT, Claude, Gemini) are becoming increasingly adopted in workflows, we are asking each of you to submit 2 Google forms recording your use of generative models in the reproducibility process. We want to understand how people are using these tools, whether these LLMs are being used properly, and more importantly, are they helpful in an educational or project setting.
